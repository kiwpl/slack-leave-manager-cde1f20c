Vacation and Sick Day Policy

Please review this policy before submitting a request.


Vacation Requests


  • All vacation requests must be submitted through this app.
  • Vacation requests are not approved until the employee receives an approval confirmation.
  • Submitting a request does not guarantee approval.
  • Vacation requests should be submitted as early as possible to support scheduling and coverage.
  • Approval may depend on staffing needs, timing, and overlapping requests from other employees.

    • Sick Day Requests


  • Sick days are intended for unexpected illness-related absences.
  • Sick day requests may only be submitted for today or a past date.
  • Future sick day submissions are not allowed.
  • Eligible sick day requests are automatically recorded in the system.
  • Misuse of sick day submissions may be reviewed by management.

    • Changes and Cancellations


  • Employees may edit or cancel their own requests in the app.
  • Changes to approved vacation requests will require re-approval.
  • Employees may cancel approved vacation requests without approval, but management will be notified.
  • Cancelled approved requests will be removed from the shared calendar.

    • Employee Responsibility


  • Employees are responsible for submitting accurate information.
  • Duplicate, misleading, or overlapping requests may be reviewed, corrected, or denied.

    • Policy Updates


  • The company may update this policy from time to time.

    • Acknowledgment


    By submitting a request, you confirm that the information provided is accurate and that you have read and understood this policy.
